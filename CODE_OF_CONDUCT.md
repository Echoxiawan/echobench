# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

EchoBench 希望成为一个对所有人友好的技术社区。参与 Issue、Pull Request、Discussions 或其他项目空间时，请遵守以下约定。

## 期望行为

- 尊重不同经验、背景和观点；
- 对事不对人，提供可复现的信息和建设性反馈；
- 接受维护者基于项目范围和质量的取舍；
- 在公开渠道讨论技术问题，在私密渠道报告安全问题；
- 不发布他人的隐私、设备信息、日志中的令牌或未授权的内容。

## 不可接受行为

- 人身攻击、侮辱、骚扰、歧视或威胁；
- 发布色情、暴力、仇恨或违法内容；
- 未经授权访问他人的设备、账号、数据或服务；
- 恶意刷屏、持续打断讨论、冒充他人或伪造身份；
- 在公开渠道披露安全漏洞细节或他人的敏感信息。

## 执行

维护者可以删除、编辑或隐藏违反本准则的内容，并视情况警告、暂时禁言或永久禁止参与。维护者应尽量说明处理理由，但不需要披露举报人的身份。

## 报告

如果你遭遇或目击违反本准则的行为，请通过维护者的 GitHub 主页私信联系，或在 GitHub 支持私密举报的入口提交。不要在公开 Issue 中披露敏感细节。

维护者会尽快审阅并采取适当措施。恶意举报或滥用举报渠道同样可能被处理。

本准则参考 Contributor Covenant 的社区规范，并针对本项目做了简化。

# English

EchoBench aims to be a welcoming technical community. When participating in Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, or other project spaces, follow these expectations.

## Expected behavior

- Respect different experience, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Critique ideas, not people; provide reproducible information and constructive feedback.
- Accept maintainers' scope and quality decisions.
- Discuss technical issues publicly and report security issues privately.
- Do not publish other people's private information, device details, tokens from logs, or unauthorized content.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, harassment, discrimination, or threats.
- Sexual, violent, hateful, or illegal content.
- Unauthorized access to another person's devices, accounts, data, or services.
- Spamming, repeatedly disrupting discussions, impersonation, or identity fraud.
- Publicly disclosing security vulnerability details or other people's sensitive information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or hide content that violates this Code, and may warn, temporarily mute, or permanently ban participants as appropriate. Maintainers should explain decisions when practical but are not required to disclose the identity of a reporter.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, contact the maintainers privately through their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's private reporting channel if available. Do not disclose sensitive details in a public Issue.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and take appropriate action. Malicious reports or abuse of the reporting process may also be handled.
